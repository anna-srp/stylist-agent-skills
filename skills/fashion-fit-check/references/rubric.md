# Fit Check Rubric

Score only visible styling evidence. Use 1–10 with half-point increments for dimensions and one decimal for the weighted overall score.

| Dimension | Weight | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Color harmony | 20% | palette relationship, contrast, repetition, and dominance |
| Proportion and layering | 20% | garment lengths, volume distribution, waist placement, and layer interaction |
| Style cohesion | 20% | whether pieces support one aesthetic and level of finish |
| Occasion fit | 15% | practicality and formality; mark `not scored` when no occasion is known |
| Overall impact | 25% | clarity, intention, focal point, and memorability |

When occasion fit is not scored, redistribute its weight proportionally across the other four dimensions. Never infer skin undertone or body type from a photo.

## Calibration

- `9.0–10`: exceptional and intentional; do not invent criticism.
- `8.0–8.9`: strong; one refinement could sharpen it.
- `7.0–7.9`: good foundation with one visible weak link.
- `6.0–6.9`: wearable but unresolved or overly safe.
- `5.0–5.9`: multiple pieces compete or the occasion logic is weak.
- `<5.0`: a clear styling reset is more useful than small tweaks.

Warm language does not require an inflated score. Use the full scale.

Good roast targets include competing colors, confused formality, an accessory doing too much, weak focal point, awkward garment interaction, or a generic combination. Forbidden targets include weight, body shape, face, skin, age, disability, gender expression, perceived income, attractiveness, or personality.
